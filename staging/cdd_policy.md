# Customer Due Diligence Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out the minimum customer due diligence requirements for all
relationships established by the institution.

## Section 1: Thresholds

The standard customer due diligence threshold is USD 15,000 for one-off
transactions. Below that amount, simplified due diligence may apply where the
risk assessment supports it.

## Section 2: Enhanced Due Diligence

Enhanced due diligence is required for politically exposed persons (PEPs) and
for transactions exceeding USD 50,000. Refer to the Enhanced Due Diligence
Policy for the specific measures required.
